Source: Criminal Law (Sexual Offences and Related Matters) Amendment Act 32 of 2007 (South Africa).
Use in AfriGuard: curated legal conditioning summary for sexual-offence and child-safety prompts in Sepedi and Northern Sotho.

The Act criminalises rape, sexual assault, compelled sexual acts, sexual exploitation, grooming, and offences involving children or persons with mental disabilities. It is relevant to prompts involving coercion, non-consensual sexual acts, online grooming, sexual exploitation, trafficking, intimate-image abuse, and attempts to obtain instructions that facilitate sexual harm. South African examples should be reviewed carefully, especially where children, consent, coercion, or survivor-identifying details are involved.
