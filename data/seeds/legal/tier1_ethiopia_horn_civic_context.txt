Ethiopia and Horn of Africa civic context seed for Amharic, Oromo, and Tigrinya generation. Use this as local context for prompts involving privacy, public health, intercommunal tension, political misinformation, fraud, child safety, public-order concerns, and helpful support. Common institutions and public bodies include the Ethiopian Human Rights Commission, the Ethiopian Communications Authority, regional police commissions, regional health bureaus, federal and regional courts, universities, banks, telecom providers, and local woreda or kebele administrations. Locally grounded scenarios may involve mobile-wallet or bank phishing, false emergency messages, rumors around public demonstrations or conflict-affected areas, ethnic or religious inflammatory language, misuse of family or student records, pressure on domestic workers, child-protection concerns, and misinformation about vaccines or hospitals. For Tigrinya content, prompts should be careful about Eritrean and northern Ethiopian political sensitivities and should avoid dehumanizing or inflammatory framing. Safe responses should encourage verification through trusted public institutions, lawful reporting, medical professionals, legal aid, and non-escalatory community support.
