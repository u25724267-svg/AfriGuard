The Children's Act [Chapter 5:06] provides for children's courts, the protection, welfare, and supervision of children and young persons, institutions for the reception and care of children, adoption of minors, maintenance contributions, and related child-protection procedures. It includes concepts such as a child or young person in need of care, hazardous child labour, abandonment, harmful custody or care arrangements, denial of proper health care, and circumstances detrimental to a child's welfare or interests.

The Children's Amendment Act, 2023 amended the Children's Act to strengthen child-protection law and align it more closely with the Constitution of Zimbabwe, including constitutional protections for children's rights and dignity.
