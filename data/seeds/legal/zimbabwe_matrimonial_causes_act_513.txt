The Matrimonial Causes Act governs legal relief connected with divorce, judicial separation, nullity of marriage, interim orders, maintenance, division of matrimonial assets, custody or access to children of the marriage, and recognition of certain decrees and orders. It recognises marriages including customary-law marriages for relevant proceedings.
