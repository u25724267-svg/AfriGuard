The Public Order and Security Act [Chapter 11:17] was a Zimbabwean public-order and security law that commenced in 2002. It is historical context only: POSA was repealed on 15 November 2019 by the Maintenance of Peace and Order Act [Chapter 11:23].

The Maintenance of Peace and Order Act makes provision for maintaining peace, order, and security in Zimbabwe, including the peaceful conduct of gatherings in a way that protects freedom of assembly, association, demonstration, and petitioning while respecting the rights and freedoms of others. It covers public gatherings, public demonstrations, public meetings, processions, regulating authorities, identity-document checks in specified public-order contexts, stop-and-search powers, and circumstances in which defence forces may assist police.
