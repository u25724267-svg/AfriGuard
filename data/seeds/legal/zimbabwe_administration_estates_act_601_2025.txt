The Administration of Estates Act concerns the administration of estates of deceased persons, minors, persons with mental incapacity, and persons absent from Zimbabwe, as well as control of money belonging to persons whose whereabouts are unknown. It provides institutional and procedural context around the Master of the High Court, estate notices, wills, movable and immovable property, surviving spouses, children, and estate administration.
