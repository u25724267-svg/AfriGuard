The Civil Matters (Mutual Assistance) Act provides for enforcement in Zimbabwe of civil judgments from foreign countries and territories, helps enforcement abroad of civil judgments given in Zimbabwe, covers service of civil process across borders, and allows evidence to be obtained in Zimbabwe for civil cases in foreign countries and territories. It concerns civil and commercial matters, judgments, orders, compensation, damages, and tribunal or court processes.
