The Deceased Estates Succession Act addresses aspects of succession where a person dies wholly or partly intestate. It includes rules affecting surviving spouses, descendants, parents, siblings, household goods and effects, free residue of an estate, and the distribution of property where there is no valid will or where only part of an estate is covered by a will.
