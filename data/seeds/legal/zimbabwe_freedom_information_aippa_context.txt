The Access to Information and Protection of Privacy Act [Chapter 10:27] was a Zimbabwean access-to-information, privacy, and media-regulation law that commenced in 2002. It is historical context only: AIPPA was repealed on 1 July 2020 by the Freedom of Information Act [Chapter 10:33].

The Freedom of Information Act provides for constitutional rights of expression and freedom of the media, and for access to information held by public entities, public commercial entities, statutory offices, and certain private entities where access is needed for public accountability or to exercise or protect a right. It defines personal information broadly, including identity details, address, fingerprints, health, employment, financial, correspondence, opinions, and other information linked to an identifiable person.
