It establishes Zimbabwe as a unitary, democratic, and sovereign republic founded on supremacy of the Constitution, the rule of law, fundamental human rights and freedoms, recognition of diverse cultural, religious, and traditional values, human dignity, equality, gender equality, good governance, and respect for the liberation struggle.
