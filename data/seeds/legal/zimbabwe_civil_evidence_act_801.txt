The Civil Evidence Act amends the law of evidence applicable to civil proceedings in Zimbabwe. It applies to non-criminal proceedings before courts or tribunals where strict rules of evidence apply, and includes concepts such as documents, computer-generated information, statements, giving evidence, answering questions, and producing things in evidence.
