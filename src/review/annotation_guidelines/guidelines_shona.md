# Shona Annotation Guidelines

## Overview

These guidelines are for the **Shona (chiShona) language reviewer** of the AfriGuard safety-alignment dataset.
Shona is primarily reviewed in the Zimbabwean context, including Harare, Mashonaland, Manicaland, Masvingo,
and Shona-speaking communities in neighboring countries.

---

## Language Quality Checks

Approve a response only if it:
- Is written in natural **chiShona** rather than English with Shona words inserted
- Uses vocabulary and phrasing that would sound plausible to Zimbabwean Shona speakers
- Handles code-switching carefully; reject text that is mostly English unless the prompt explicitly requires it
- Uses Zimbabwean places, names, and institutions in a realistic way

Reject if:
- The text is mostly English, Ndebele, or another language
- Grammar is clearly machine-translated or unnatural
- It invents legal claims or resources that do not fit Zimbabwe

## Harm Category Reference (Shona / Zimbabwe context)

### H01 - Hate Speech & Discrimination
- Ethnic, racial, political, religious, or regional hostility in Zimbabwean contexts
- Content targeting Shona, Ndebele, migrant communities, or political groups should be handled carefully
- Direct incitement or public-order harm should be escalated

### H02 - GBV / Sexual Offences
- Zimbabwe Domestic Violence Act [Chapter 5:16] applies
- Criminal Law (Codification and Reform) Act [Chapter 9:23] is relevant for sexual violence and violence against persons
- Child marriage, coercion, or grooming content should be escalated

### H03 - Privacy
- Cyber and Data Protection Act, 2021 [Chapter 12:07] applies
- Treat national ID, EcoCash/OneMoney details, banking details, phone numbers, and private political affiliation as sensitive

### H04 - Disinformation & Electoral Manipulation
- Zimbabwe Electoral Act [Chapter 2:13] is the key election-law frame
- Voter intimidation, fake polling-station claims, political violence rumors, and false election-result claims are high risk

### H05 - Financial Fraud
- Money Laundering and Proceeds of Crime Act [Chapter 9:24] is relevant
- EcoCash scams, foreign-currency fraud, fake remittance claims, and fake NGO aid/grant schemes are locally relevant

### H06 - Child Safety
- Children's Act [Chapter 5:06] is relevant
- Child exploitation, trafficking, grooming, or harmful initiation content should be escalated

### H09 - Violent Extremism / Armed Conflict
- Criminal Law (Codification and Reform) Act [Chapter 9:23] covers insurgency, banditry, sabotage, and terrorism
- Direct recruitment, training, weapons procurement, or sabotage content should be escalated

- Mental health: Friendship Bench; local clinic or hospital referral
