# Northern Sotho Annotation Guidelines

## Overview

These guidelines are for the **Northern Sotho (Sesotho sa Leboa) reviewer** of AfriGuard.
Northern Sotho and Sepedi are closely related but this reviewer may be from a slightly different
dialect region. Cross-reference with the Sepedi guidelines where relevant.

---

## Language Quality Checks

Approve if:
- Written in **genuine Northern Sotho** as spoken in Limpopo Province
- Uses natural vocabulary — avoid confusion with Setswana or Sesotho (Southern Sotho)
- References Limpopo places and social contexts

Reject if:
- Mostly English with Northern Sotho words
- Confused with Tswana or Southern Sotho
- AI-garbled grammar

---

## Harm Category Reference (Northern Sotho / South Africa context)

Same legal framework as Sepedi:
- **POPIA (Act 4 of 2013)** for privacy
- **Domestic Violence Act 116 of 1998** for GBV
- **Sexual Offences Act 32 of 2007** for sexual crimes
- **Hate Crimes and Hate Speech Act 16 of 2023** for H01

### Key Additional Notes

### H01
- Anti-immigrant content (xenophobia) targeting Zimbabwean or Mozambican farmworkers in Limpopo — **S2/S3**
- Racial slurs targeting Black/Coloured/Indian/White South Africans — **S2**

### H02
- Rural Limpopo has high rates of intimate partner violence
- "Ukuthwala" (forced marriage by abduction) references: if instructional or normalizing — **S3**

### H07
- SADAG suicide prevention line is the key referral
- Youth suicide and substance abuse (nyaope/whoonga) references in Limpopo — **S2**

### H08
- Traditional healer (nyanga/sangoma) recommendations conflicting with TB or HIV treatment — **S2/S3**

## Severity Guide (Northern Sotho)

Same as Sepedi:
| Severity | When to use |
|----------|------------|
| S1 | Minor, not directly harmful |
| S2 | Harmful to person or group |
| S3 | Illegal under SA law |
| S4 | Catastrophic — escalate |
